Pricing questions

Before you decide.

What does it cost?

Starter websites begin at $100 for a simple one-page site. The Website Foundation, a professional multi-page website, starts from $250. A Connected Business System (CRM, WhatsApp booking, workflows) starts from $500, and Business Automation (admin portals, automations, reporting) is quoted to scope. Prices are in USD — message us on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

What is included in the starting website package?

The $100 Starter Website is a one-page mobile-first website with your business information and services, a basic WhatsApp contact, basic SEO metadata, SSL security, a sitemap, Google Search Console submission and full ownership of the site. The Website Foundation, from $250, is a multi-page website with WhatsApp integration, technical SEO and AEO foundations, Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools setup, Cloudflare where applicable, Google Business Profile alignment, analytics and full ownership of your site and data. Additional languages and full Google Business Profile optimisation are quoted separately.

How much does a website cost in Kenya or Tanzania?

For businesses in Kenya and Tanzania, UmojaServ Starter websites begin at $100 USD, the Website Foundation starts from $250 and a Connected Business System (Google Sheets CRM, WhatsApp booking) starts from $500. Business Automation (admin portals, automations) is quoted to scope. Final pricing depends on scope, content, integrations and workflow — message UmojaServ on WhatsApp with what your business does for an exact quote.

Is UmojaServ better than Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress for a small business in Africa?

UmojaServ and platforms like Wix, Squarespace, and WordPress solve the problem differently: those are self-service platforms you build and maintain yourself on a subscription, while UmojaServ is a built-for-you static system with no monthly platform fee. Add a Connected Business System, and your bookings and customer records live in your own Google account. UmojaServ suits an owner who wants the system built and handed over; the platforms suit someone who would rather build and manage it themselves.
