How we scored this

Every score comes from fixed rules, not opinion. We apply the same scoring rules every time, and an AI never changes a number. Results can change when your website, test conditions or answers change. This is an UmojaServ score, not an official Google or AI-platform rating. It does not guarantee rankings, citations or sales.

Where the information comes from

  • Google PageSpeed: how fast and stable your site is on a phone.
  • Website analysis: what we can read on your public pages, such as links, buttons, titles and contact details.
  • Structured website data: the business details your site states in a form machines can read.
  • Information provided by the business: your answers to the quick questions. We never look up review sites.

The categories

  • Website Performance: PageSpeed mobile score, load speed, layout stability and responsiveness.
  • Mobile Experience: phone-friendly setup, navigation, tap targets, readability, a call or message button, and forms.
  • Google Search Readiness measures whether your website provides the technical, structural and local signals that help search engines understand and index your business. It does not measure your actual Google ranking or Search Console performance.
  • UmojaServ AI Search Readiness measures how clearly your business information can be understood by search engines and AI systems. It does not guarantee placement or citation by any AI platform.
  • WhatsApp Conversion: whether a customer can start a useful WhatsApp conversation from your site, and whether you can tell where it came from. The weight reflects UmojaServ's main markets, where WhatsApp is the main channel.
  • Local SEO: your name, address and phone, local structured data, city mentions, map links and hours. We do not check your Google Business Profile.
  • Reviews, Customer Follow-up and Automation come from your answers. They stay "Not assessed yet" until you answer.

What is left out of a score

If we could not measure something, have not asked yet, or it does not apply to your type of business, it is left out and the rest is re-weighted. It is never counted as zero. A low score only means we measured it and found room to improve.

The overall score

The Digital Business Score is a weighted average of the categories we could assess. Before you answer the quick questions it is shown as provisional. Category scores matter more than the single number.

Scoring version 1.0.0. Scores from different versions should not be compared. UmojaServ can share the full scoring rules on request.
