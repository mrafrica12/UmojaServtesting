Umoja in Action

Technology is only part of the story.

Umoja means unity in Swahili. Serv is for service to others. We take both halves of the name seriously.

Alongside the digital systems we build, UmojaServ supports young people and communities through football, mentorship and donated equipment — in Kenya, Zanzibar, Ghana, Cuba and beyond. I've coached youth soccer since 2005. Helping where I can came long before the websites did.

Football & youth

Football brings people together.

Football is at the heart of UmojaServ's community work. Over the years, we have worked closely with youth clubs and communities — from training grounds in Kenya to fields in Cuba.

Mentorship

Sport and education, together.

Alongside football, UmojaServ supports young people through mentorship. Time on the sideline can be time for learning too.

Equipment & support

Donated gear, put to use.

UmojaServ has donated soccer gear to youth clubs and programmes, including Blue Bentos FC, a youth football club in Nairobi.

We also built Blue Bentos FC's website — the same work we do for businesses, put to use for the club.
