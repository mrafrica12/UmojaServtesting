Getting Started

How long does it take to build a website?

A Website Foundation can go live in as little as 48 hours from when we start the build, once we have what we need from you. Connected Business Systems and automation take longer — the timeline depends on the integrations and workflow involved, and we agree it with you before we start.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use this?

No. UmojaServ is built around tools you already use — WhatsApp and Google Sheets. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can run your system once a CRM or booking workflow is set up.

Who is UmojaServ for?

Small and growing businesses across East Africa and the African diaspora — including entrepreneurs in Atlanta and across the United States — who are tired of juggling five apps to serve one customer.

Why should I choose UmojaServ?

Because East African and diaspora small businesses are who we build for. The systems are simple enough to use on a busy day. A website can launch in as little as 48 hours, in English, Swahili or other languages. And it runs on tools you already own, so your data stays yours.

Why does my business need a website if my competitors don't have one?

It depends where you are. In the U.S., 83% of small businesses have a website (Clutch, 2025). In East Africa it varies a lot. The World Bank's latest Enterprise Surveys put it at about 74% in Kenya (2025), 34% in Tanzania (2023) and 32% in Uganda (2025). Those surveys only count registered firms with five or more employees, so smaller businesses aren't in those numbers. Either way, having a website isn't the same as being connected. A site nobody finds, that doesn't link to your WhatsApp or keep track of who asked what, doesn't do much. That's the part we build: website, Google, WhatsApp, customer records, payments and automation, working together.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp with a bit about your business. We handle the build — a website can go live in as little as 48 hours, and connected systems are scoped around your workflow.

SEO and AI Visibility

What is the difference between SEO and AI Visibility™?

SEO helps people find your business through search engines such as Google and Bing. AI Visibility™ is about being described correctly when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews for a recommendation. We write clear business information, FAQs and structured data those tools can read.

UmojaServ improves this foundation through clear website content, accurate business details, helpful FAQs and structured data where appropriate. These improvements support discovery, but do not guarantee rankings, citations or recommendations.

Our Website Foundation includes technical SEO and AEO foundations. The $100 Starter Website has a smaller scope, as described in our pricing.

Can AI bring customers to my business?

What tools does UmojaServ offer?

Every Website Foundation includes four essential tools, because we believe every business website should have the foundation needed to be found online, attract customers, and grow: Google Business Profile alignment, Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools, Cloudflare (where applicable), and Google Search Console.

What is Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools?

It helps monitor your website's performance on Bing, identify SEO opportunities, submit sitemaps, and improve search visibility.

Do you submit my website to Microsoft Bing?

UmojaServ submits every Website Foundation and higher-level build to Microsoft Bing, not just Google. Each of these sites includes Bing Webmaster Tools setup and verification (via DNS, meta tag, XML file, or importing straight from Google Search Console), XML sitemap submission, indexing requests, and ongoing SEO performance monitoring — clicks, impressions, keyword data, crawl info, Core Web Vitals, and site scans. One note: we implement IndexNow on your site so it can instantly notify Bing of new or updated pages — Bing then decides what to do with that signal on their end, which is outside anyone's control but theirs.

What is Google Search Console?

It tracks how your website performs in Google Search, helps identify technical issues, monitors search traffic, and improves SEO performance.

Do you set up a sitemap.xml and robots.txt for my site?

Yes. Every UmojaServ website includes a sitemap.xml, which lists all your pages so search engines like Google and Bing can find and index them faster, and a robots.txt file, which tells search engines which parts of your site to crawl. Together, they help your website get found and ranked correctly from day one.

Will my website appear on Google Search?

Yes. Every website includes a sitemap and Google Search Console submission so it can be found and indexed on Google Search. The Website Foundation adds full Google Search Console setup and Google Business Profile alignment, so your business shows consistently on Google Search and Google Maps.
