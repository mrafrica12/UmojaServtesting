Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

UmojaServ ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website or services.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your business name and type
  • Your country of operation
  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • A description of your business goal

We do not collect payment information, passwords, or any sensitive personal data through our website form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Contact you via WhatsApp to discuss your project
  • Understand your business needs and provide relevant services
  • Improve our service offerings
  • Manage our internal CRM and follow-up processes

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your form submissions are stored securely in Google Sheets, protected by Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure. We access this data exclusively through our internal admin portal to serve you better.

4. Communications

By submitting our contact form, you consent to being contacted by UmojaServ via WhatsApp. You may opt out of communications at any time by messaging us directly on WhatsApp.

5. Cookies

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies only (such as your language preference). We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (no personal data collected)
  • WhatsApp — as our primary communication channel
  • Google Sheets / Apps Script — for secure data storage

6a. Business Digital Health Check

When you use our free Business Digital Health Check, we read the public pages of the website address you enter, and we send that address to Google PageSpeed Insights to measure mobile speed. The results are shown in your browser. We do not store the website address, your results or your answers to the optional questions unless you choose to send them to us. We keep only short technical logs (for example a random reference and whether the check worked), not the website address or your answers.

If you ask for the complete report, or send your results to us on WhatsApp, we receive what you choose to share: your name and business name, your email address or WhatsApp number, the website address, your business type and city, the scores shown, the top recommendations and your consent. We use this only to contact you about your report and our services, and you can ask us to delete it at any time (see section 7). The Health Check page measures how the tool is used with anonymous events such as “started” and “completed”. These contain no website address, business details, answers or scores.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly via WhatsApp.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by applicable law. If you request deletion, we will remove your data within 14 days.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our admin portal is password-protected and access is restricted to authorised UmojaServ team members only.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
