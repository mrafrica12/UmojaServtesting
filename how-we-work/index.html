How we work

Build. Test. Optimise. Measure.

Every website we build is tested on mobile and keeps being improved after launch.

Measured performance

Built fast. Then measured.

We test the websites we build using Google PageSpeed Insights and continue optimising them after launch.

  1. BuildCreate the mobile-first website and connected business system.
  2. TestTest mobile performance, usability and technical SEO.
  3. OptimiseImprove assets, loading behaviour and delivery while preserving the customer experience.
  4. IndexConnect search infrastructure such as Google Search Console and sitemaps.
  5. Hand overWalk you and your team through the system, train them on the workflow and, for admin or staff portals, write an illustrated SOP manual from your own screens.
  6. MeasureTrack search visibility, Google Business Profile discovery, WhatsApp sources, form submissions, CTA activity and eventually CRM conversion.
